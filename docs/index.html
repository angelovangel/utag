ONT kits — sequence 5′ of the insert

  • All kits share the start of the sequencing adapter, CCTGTACTTCGTTCAGTT. Ligation kits continue with ACGTATTGCT (the Ligation Adapter in the ONT protocols); the rapid adapter continues with a 5–11 nt stretch that basecalls variably (most often ATGCAAAA), then GCTTGGGTGTTTAACC.
  • Rapid kits (RAD / RBK) cut the amplicons at random with a transposase, so the utags are rarely at a read end; utag needs a ligation kit (LSK / NBD) or MAB.
  • utag context names the kit from these sequences, and its ONT barcodes, when no utag is found.
